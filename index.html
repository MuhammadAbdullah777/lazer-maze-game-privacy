Privacy Policy

Laser Maze: Neon Prism • Package: com.prismgrid.laser_maze • Effective: November 15, 2026

1. Overview

Laser Maze: Neon Prism (“the App”) is an offline puzzle game published by Prism Grid Studio. Your privacy matters to us. The App does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, sell, or share any personal information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or any other personal details.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics, or crash-tracking data.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

To let you continue playing, the App saves your game progress (levels completed, stars earned, best moves) and your settings (such as vibration on/off) in a small local database on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server. It is deleted when you use “Reset Progress” in Settings or uninstall the App.

4. Internet, Ads and Third Parties

The App works fully offline and does not request internet access. It contains no advertisements, no in-app purchases, and no third-party SDKs that collect user data.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because no personal information is collected from anyone, including children, the App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Data Security

Since all data stays on your device, it is protected by your device’s own security features (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same page and the effective date above will be revised. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

8. Your Consent

By downloading and using Laser Maze: Neon Prism, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
